SECTION 01 — INTRODUCTION

Introduction

Welcome to the technical reference manual for emergancy submarine escape. This document contains all information about helping submarine opperatives escape when power is cut to the machine.

Please read through each module carefully. All information has been organised into individual modules for ease of reference. You are the expert the player in the submarine is counting on you to provide correct and accurate information. Don't let them down.

IMPORTANT NOTICE
Always ensure that you are referring to the correct edition of this document before proceeding. This verion of the document is for game version: 0.1.

SECTION 02 — INFORMATION ABOUT THE SUBMARINE

Information About The Submarine

This section provides an overview of the equipment provide to the escapee inside of the submarine.

Modules

Each submarine will have many possible modules inside of it. By solving these modules the escapee will begin to power on the espace hatch at the top of the submarine. After solving all of the modules the escapee can climb out of the hatch and swim up to the sruface.

Errors

In every submarine, the escapee can have up to three errors before they overload the spare fuse and the hatch is closed forever.

Extra Information

There will be some modules that require the escapee to gather information around the submarine. If there is ever a time where you need information that you don't have, then ask the escapee to look around for it, you have no idea what you'll find.

Reference Notes

SECTION 03 — Modules

On The Subject Of Fish

The escapee is presented with four buttons, each with different pictures of fish on them. Push the buttons in the correct order to supply power. There will be a fish tank on board the submarine. The number of fish and their colour will tell you how which order to push the buttons in.

2 fish:
If there are no red fish, press the blue button, then yellow, then green, then red
Otherwise, press the red button, then green, then yellow, then blue
3 fish:
If there is a white fish and no yellow fish, press the yellow button, then green, then blue, then red
Otherwise, if there is a pink fish and no green fish, press the green button, then red, then blue, then yellow
Otherwise, press the red button, then green, then blue, then yellow
4 fish:
If there is a yellow fish and a green fish, then press the green button, then blue, then red, then yellow
Otherwise, if there is a red fish and a blue fish, and there isn't a white fish, press the yellow button, then blue, then red, then green
Otherwise, if there is a pink fish and there isn't a white fish, press the blue button, then green, then yellow, then red
Otherwise, press the red button, then blue, then green, then yellow
5+ fish:
If there are 6 fish, press the red button, then blue, then yellow, then green
Otherwise, if three of the fish are cold colours, then press the blue button, then green, then red, then yellow
Otherwise, if there is a white fish and a blue fish, then press the green button, then yellow, then red, then blue
Otherwise, press the yellow button, then blue, then green, then red
